Brief

The Brief button (keyboard shortcut B on a selected row) generates the briefing text for a session and shows it in a dialog so you can review or copy it manually.

When to use it

  • You want to re-paste the briefing into a session that's already running.
  • You want to inspect what Foreman will send before launching.
  • You're on a platform where Launch is not available and need to paste manually.

Automatic briefing on Launch

When you click Launch, the briefing is copied to the clipboard automatically — you don't need to open the Brief dialog first. Brief is the standalone version of that same copy step.
